How It Works

1. Capture Everything

Quickly add tasks to your Inbox via keyboard shortcut, menu bar, or drag-and-drop. Attach links and assign preliminary categories or projects as needed.

2. Process and Organize

Review Inbox items: mark as Actionable, Waiting For, or Someday. Link to projects, set priorities, due dates, and contexts for contextual clarity.

3. Execute with Focus

Use Today for immediate priorities and Next for upcoming actions. Drag tasks across views in Kanban or list mode to adapt on the fly.

4. Review and Refine

Weekly: Scan Priorities and Dates views. Reorganize projects, clear Waiting For items, and archive completed tasks to keep your system trusted.
